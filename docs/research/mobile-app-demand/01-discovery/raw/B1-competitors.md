# B1: Direct Competitor Deep-Dives (native app idea for スミハジメ)

Research date: 2026-09-30. Read-only web research using the iTunes Search/Lookup/RSS APIs (JP storefront), Google Play JP listing pages, official sites and press releases.
Labels: [Data] = observed at the cited URL on 2026-09-30 unless dated otherwise; [Estimate] = derived from data (method stated); [Assumption] = not verified; [Opinion] = analyst judgement.
Staleness rule: anything dated before 2025-03-30 is marked **STALE (>18 months)**.
Quoting: store reviews are paraphrased. Only one short direct quote is used.

---

## 0. Headline

1. **No private competitor produces a per-municipality, source-cited, deadline-ordered checklist.** [Data, from every store description and site checked below] Every private app uses one national task template of about 60 tasks. The best of them add statutory deadlines such as 転入届 14日. None shows ward-specific procedures, and none gives an official source with a last-verified date for each task.
2. **The municipality-accurate niche is taken by official, single-municipality web tools.** Graffer 手続きガイド runs a 転入ガイド for at least 8 Tokyo municipalities. マイナポータル handles 転出届 and 来庁予約. The 東京アプリ has 5M downloads and already links to ward e-application sites and to 引越れんらく帳. [Data]
3. **No moving-checklist app has reached scale. The category's traction is weak.** The best dedicated app, らくらくMOVING, has about 650 iOS ratings over 4.5 years and a 1万+ Google Play install band. SUUMO引越しダンドリ (Recruit) and アート引越センター's 「単身引越スイスイ！お見積り」 were both withdrawn from the stores. [Data] Large players have retreated to web plus lead generation (quote-request referrals). [Opinion]
4. **The checklist-plus-reminders feature is now a commodity.** At least 10 new indie iOS apps shipped between 2025-10 and 2026-09 with almost the same pitch: questions, then a checklist in deadline order, then reminders. A few even cite the statute behind each deadline. Each has 0 to 2 ratings. [Data]

## 1. Competitor profiles

### 1-1. らくらくMOVING (引越しやること手続き管理) — the strongest dedicated private app

- **Operator:** SAILING PAY FORWARD CO., LTD. (Yokohama). [Data]
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android. iOS released 2022-02-01. Latest iOS version 1.6.2, updated 2026-01-20. Google Play last updated 2025-12-17. [Data] https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1597858051 , https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.raku_m
- **What it does:**
  - 9–10 questions select from 60+ generic tasks ("missions").
  - Push reminders and custom tasks.
  - A postal-code search for the relevant 役所, 警察署, 保健所 and 免許センター, with Google Maps.
  - Free 代行手配 (arranged sign-ups) for electricity, gas and internet.
  - Quotes from about 60 moving companies. [Data, store description]
- **Municipal deadlines by municipality:** No. It only finds facility locations. Tasks are generic. [Data]
- **Personalization inputs:** new postal code, move date, username, gender, and 9–10 questions such as car ownership. [Data]
- **Official sources:** No. Links go to its own media and database. [Data]
- **Monetization:** The app is free with no ads. Revenue almost certainly comes from utility and moving-company referral fees: staff phone the user after a 代行手配 request. [Data for the 代行 mechanism; Assumption for the fee]
- **Traction:**
  - iOS: 4.6★ from 650 ratings. Google Play: 4.1★ from 69 reviews, 1万+ installs. [Data]
  - [Estimate] About 20k–65k iOS installs, assuming ratings are 1–3% of installs. Adding the Google Play band gives roughly 30k–115k lifetime installs, or about 7k–25k a year.
- **Complaints (paraphrased):**
  - The app gets stuck after the first input screen (iOS, 2024-05 and 2026-01).
  - The list cannot be reset, so it is useless for a second move.
  - Bottom-menu buttons are hard to tap on devices with 3-button navigation (Google Play, 2026-03).
  - Edit and back labels are cut off (Google Play, 2026-01).
- **Maintained?** Barely. Updates are minor ("軽微な修正"). [Data]

### 1-2. 引越し手続きガイド (hikkoshi-guide.jp) — the oldest dedicated app, a lead-generation funnel

- **Operator:** PATHFINDER, K.K. [Data]
- **Platforms:** iOS (released 2018-12-25, last update 2025-09-05), Android (last update 2026-05-16) and web. [Data] https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id1447039435 , https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.p_finder.android.hikkoshiguide , https://www.hikkoshi-guide.jp/
- **What it does:** 5–8 questions (household size, car or bike, children, pets) produce a checklist and countdown, a list of places to contact, and a bulk quote request to moving companies. [Data]
- **Municipal deadlines by municipality:** No. Generic list only. [Data]
- **Official sources:** No. [Data]
- **Monetization:** Ads (Google Play says 「広告が表示されます」) plus leads for moving companies. [Data]
- **Traction:** iOS 3.4★ from 52 ratings. Google Play 3.8★ from 30 reviews, 1万+ installs. [Data]
- **Complaints (paraphrased):**
  - After requesting quotes, users got floods of phone calls from moving and internet companies, and even unrelated sales calls (2021-12, 2022-04, 2023-03).
  - One user received a bill on LINE from a company they had never used (2023-01).
  - Missing features: users cannot add custom TODOs, and there is no 転出届 item (2022-01).
  - All of these reviews are **STALE (>18 months)**, but they show a structural weakness of the lead-generation model.
- **Maintained?** Occasional updates. [Data]

- **Monetization:** Funnel into the ミツモア marketplace (moving and cleaning). [Data]
- **Traction:** Google Play 2.3★ from 10 reviews, 5,000+ installs. iOS 4.25★ from 4 ratings. [Data]

- **Traction:** マイナアプリ on Google Play: 1,000万+ installs, 2.0★ from 1.69万 reviews, updated 2026-09-09. iOS: 2.18★ from 988 ratings, updated 2026-09-29. [Data] https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.go.cas.mpa
- **Moving-procedure volume:** **DATA GAP.** Digital Agency pages say only that use is "広がっています". The マイナポータル dashboard did not show moving counts in fetchable form.

### 1-6. 東京都公式アプリ (東京アプリ) — the platform threat

- **Operator:** Tokyo Metropolitan Government / GovTech東京. [Data]
- **Platforms:** iOS (released 2025-02-16) and Android. [Data]
- **Traction:** 5M+ downloads as of 2026-03-23. iOS 3.9★ from 11,517 ratings. Google Play 3.7★ from 4,670 reviews, 100万+ installs. Growth was driven by an 11,000 東京ポイント campaign that requires マイナンバーカード verification and runs from 2026-02-02 to 2027-04-01. [Data] https://www.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/information/press/2026/03/2026032410
- **Moving relevance:**
  - Since 2026-03 the app links to each 区市町村's e-application site and official LINE after the user picks a municipality.
  - It showcases 引越れんらく帳 as seasonal content. [Data]
  - It has no checklist of its own. [Data]
  - The operator has stated it will add administrative procedures step by step. [Data]
- **Complaints (paraphrased, 2026-08/09):** Slow loading, confusing layout, broken point linkage between app and web, and hard to use without a マイナンバーカード. [Data]
- [Opinion] This is where a Tokyo-wide moving checklist would most plausibly appear if the government chose to build one. It is both a threat and a possible distribution partner.

### 1-7. 引越れんらく帳 — incumbent for utilities plus 転出届 (web only)

- **Operator:** TEPCO group. The site footer says © TEPCO Energy Partner; 2024 releases name TEPCO i-フロンティアズ. Running for 20+ years. [Data] https://www.hikkoshi-line.com/
- **What it does:**
  - One registration submits moving notices to electricity, gas, water, telecoms, newspapers and similar.
  - Since 2024-03-01 it also submits **転出届 and 転入 visit bookings for all 1,741 municipalities** via the マイナポータル API, built on NTT Data's BizMINT. [Data] https://www.nttdata.com/global/ja/news/topics/2024/022600/
- **Municipal checklist with deadlines:** No per-municipality checklist. There is a deadline reminder feature. Partner companies are concentrated in Kanto. [Data] https://enechange.jp/articles/hikkoshi-renrakucho (2026-04-16)
- **Platforms:** Web only, with registration or login required. **No native app.** [Data]
- **Traction:** The site claims 「年間12万人が利用」. [Data, self-reported]
- **Monetization:** Free to users. [Data] [Assumption] Paid by partner companies and the TEPCO group, which uses it to acquire and retain customers.
- **Complaints (paraphrased, from ENECHANGE):** Hard to use. In rural areas only TEPCO shows up. Many procedures are missing, such as 健康保険 and 運転免許. No app. [Data]

### 1-8. Graffer 手続きガイド / くらしのてつづき — official, accurate, one municipality at a time

- **Operator:** Graffer, Inc., under contract to each municipality. First deployed in Kamakura in 2018-11. [Data] https://ttzk.graffer.jp/ , https://graffer.jp/governments/solution-guides
- **Coverage:** The portal lists 187 organizations, including 20 Tokyo municipalities. A probe of the `/move-in` path confirmed **転入ガイド pages for 千代田, 文京, 品川, 目黒, 大田, 世田谷, 荒川 and 武蔵野** (all returned HTTP 200). Other municipalities may use different paths. [Data]
- **What it does:** Yes/no questions produce a list of that municipality's procedures, a 持ち物リスト (things to bring), downloadable forms, a print option and a share URL. Some guides have a 2D barcode for the counter. [Data] Example: the Setagaya 転入 guide was updated 2026-03-31. https://ttzk.graffer.jp/ward-setagaya/move-in
- **Municipal accuracy:** High, because the municipality maintains it. [Assumption]
- **Gaps:**
  - Covers only one municipality. There is no 転出元 plus 転入先 view.
  - Has no move-date-driven deadline timeline, calendar export or reminders. [Data for the absence in the page configuration; not exhaustively tested]
  - Covers no private procedures such as utilities.
  - Web only.
- **Usage:** DATA GAP.
- **Related:** Several wards run 「書かない窓口」 pre-entry services, where the resident fills in the 住民異動届 on a phone and brings a QR code: 江戸川区, 中野区, 府中市 and others. [Data] https://www.city.edogawa.tokyo.jp/e038/kurashi/todoke/ido_todoke/kakanai_info.html , https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/koseki/madoguti/ikanai-kakanai-matanai.html

## 4. Vulnerability analysis

### Where competitors are weak and a native スミハジメ could win

1. **Deadlines that are accurate for each municipality, across municipalities.** [Data] No private app goes past national statutory deadlines. Official Graffer guides are accurate but cover one municipality at a time, do not follow the user's move date and have no reminders. A native app that combines 転出元 plus 転入先, household-specific items and push reminders on the real deadline would be unique **for the 24 covered municipalities**. [Opinion]
2. **Trust and privacy against the lead-generation model.** The most repeated complaint across the category is the flood of sales calls after entering personal details (引越し手続きガイド) and ad-heavy apps that harvest data (ミツモア). A no-login, no-PII app that states it earns no referral fees is a real, visible difference on a store page. [Data for the complaints; Opinion for the win]
3. **Citations and freshness.** No competitor shows a last-verified date. Stale or inaccurate content is a known category failure: the SUUMO ダンドリ example shows 転出届 framed as "14日前まで". [Data] A per-task 「最終確認日」 is hard for code-generated clones to fake at scale because it needs an ongoing verification process.
4. **Grounded AI chat.** ミツモア's chat is uncited. A chat that answers only from official pages, with links, and declines to answer otherwise is differentiated. [Data/Opinion] Caveat: this does not require a native app.
5. **Reliable deadline reminders during the 14-day window after the move.** The web version has only calendar export. Push notifications timed to 転入届 14日, 児童手当 15日, 国保 14日 and similar would be a real functional gain. [Opinion] **This is the single strongest argument for going native** (or for adding web push to the PWA).

### Where a native app would NOT be differentiated

1. **Checklist, questions and reminders.** This is now a commodity; see §1-10. The store is saturated with near-identical apps. [Data]
2. **Discovery.** App Store searches for 引越し are dominated by property giants (SUUMO 384k ratings, HOME'S 137k). Dedicated moving apps get tiny rating counts. The moving moment is found through Google SEO, real-estate agents, ward websites and 東京アプリ, and none of these channels favors a native install over a URL. [Data for rating counts; Opinion for the channel]
3. **One-time use.** People move once every few years. There is a complaint that らくらくMOVING cannot be reset for a second move. Uninstall right after the roughly 2-month moving window is the norm. [Opinion] Retention-driven economics do not apply, and store rating accumulation will be slow.
4. **Offline and calendar.** A PWA or web offline copy plus .ics export already covers most of this. Native adds reliable push, widgets and a Wallet-like feel, and little else. [Opinion]
5. **Government platform overlap.** マイナポータル owns the 転出届 transaction. 東京アプリ owns Tokyo distribution (5M) and already points to 引越れんらく帳 and ward e-application sites. If the Tokyo government adds a moving checklist to 東京アプリ, it wins on distribution immediately. [Opinion] The better strategic position may be to be the _data/content layer_ that 東京アプリ or a ward links to, not a rival app.
6. **Coverage asymmetry.** Competitors claim nationwide coverage, even if only generically. A native app that works well in only 24 of 62 Tokyo municipalities will get 1★ reviews from people outside them, as seen with 引越れんらく帳's "only TEPCO shows up" complaint. [Opinion]

### Is there a dominant solution (10k+ users)?

- **Yes, for pieces of the job:**
  - マイナポータル / マイナアプリ (1,000万+ installs) for 転出届.
  - 引越れんらく帳 (claims 120k a year) for utilities plus 転出届.
  - 引越し侍, SUUMO and LIFULL for quotes and SEO content.
  - 東京アプリ (5M) as Tokyo's government front door.
  - [Data]
- **No, for the core job** of a per-municipality deadline checklist with official sources. The largest dedicated app, らくらくMOVING, has about 1万+ Google Play installs and 650 iOS ratings over 4.5 years ([Estimate] about 30k–115k lifetime installs, national). [Data/Estimate] The gap is real, but the weak traction of every attempt suggests **low willingness to install a dedicated app** rather than an unmet but eager demand. [Opinion]

## 5. Red and yellow flags

### Red flags

- **R1: Precedent failure by a well-resourced player.** Recruit's SUUMO引越しダンドリ was withdrawn (10k–50k Google Play installs, 3.3★). Art's estimate-plus-TODO app was replaced by a quote-only app. [Data]
- **R2: The category ceiling is low.** The best dedicated app has about 650 iOS ratings over 4.5 years. New 2026 entrants backed by established companies (CareerIndex, ミツモア) have only 500+ and 5,000+ Google Play installs. [Data]
- **R3: The features are commoditized.** At least 10 indie clones shipped in 12 months, some copying the statutory-deadline and 「要確認」 framing. A native app does not create a moat; only verified municipal data does. [Data/Opinion]
- **R4: Platform risk from the government.** 東京アプリ (5M downloads, adding administrative functions) and マイナポータル can absorb the use case. 東京アプリ already promotes 引越れんらく帳 rather than a checklist. [Data/Opinion]

### Yellow flags

- **Y1: Episodic use** means low retention and slow review accumulation, and makes push-based engagement short-lived. [Opinion]
- **Y2: The only proven revenue model** is lead-generation (utility and mover referrals), which スミハジメ's no-PII principle rules out. Any native app must be funded by grants, public partnership or sponsorship. [Data for the model; Opinion for the implication]
- **Y3: Official per-ward guides (Graffer, 書かない窓口)** already cover municipal accuracy in at least 8 Tokyo municipalities. スミハジメ's differentiation is cross-municipality coverage, deadline timing and citations, not accuracy alone. [Data]
- **Y4: Coverage of 24 of 62 municipalities** on a store that expects nationwide coverage risks bad reviews and misleading expectations (see principle #9: do not present unsupported municipalities as supported). [Opinion]
- **Y5: Weak data on government adoption.** Uptake of the マイナポータル moving service is a DATA GAP after 2023, and the 2023 survey is STALE. [Data]
- **Y6: Unreliable ratings.** Some competitor ratings may be solicited (CareerIndex), so ratings are a weak proxy for demand. [Opinion]

### DATA GAPs

- Online 転出届 volumes after 2023.
- Actual install numbers for iOS apps (only rating counts are available).
- Usage of the Graffer guides.
- Web traffic for 引越し侍, SUUMO引越し and 引越れんらく帳 checklist pages.
- The exact end dates of SUUMO引越しダンドリ and アートスイスイ.
- Whether 東京アプリ has a moving checklist on its roadmap.
- A search-budget limit was hit near the end, so 引越し侍 user totals were not retrieved.
